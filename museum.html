03 · The Museum

A public room for a quiet tradition.

The Mithila Yoga Museum is open to the public — a small, careful space that tells the story of yoga in this region for anyone who walks in. Through objects, image, sound, and word, we try to make audible a tradition that has lived more often in voice than in display.

What you'll see

A walk through the tradition.

The museum is organised loosely by period — from the Upanishadic age of Yājñavalkya and Janaka through the medieval flowering of Mithila Nyāya, the Bhāmatī school of Vedānta, the Tantric tradition of the Mahāvidyās, and into the living tradition of Vidyāpati, Chhath, and the Kohbar painting practice. Where we can, we use the original Maithili or Sanskrit alongside English so that local visitors and visitors from elsewhere can read together.
