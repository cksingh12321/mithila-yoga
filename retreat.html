01 · The Retreat Centre

A sanctuary for visiting teachers.

The Mithila Yoga Retreat Centre is offered to yoga teachers, meditation guides, and contemplative practitioners who wish to bring their students into the land where the tradition was first articulated. We do not run retreats ourselves. Instead we hold the place — the shala, the grounds, the quiet — for teachers from anywhere in the world who lead their own programmes here.

What's offered

The essentials, well-held.

  • A purpose-built yoga shala, with natural light and wooden floor
  • Accommodation for teacher and students [capacity tbd]
  • Three sattvic meals daily, drawn from local Maithil cuisine
  • Quiet grounds, walking paths, and ponds for reflection
  • Optional access to the Archives and Museum on the campus
  • Optional cultural inclusions (Madhubani art, Maithili music)
  • Airport / station pickup, arranged in advance
  • On-site staff for hospitality and practical needs

How to book

Typical bookings.

We host retreats from short weekend immersions to multi-week teacher trainings. The site is offered as a full-property buyout for the duration of your programme — your group has the shala, the cottages, the grounds, and our staff for the entire stay.

3–4 days A long-weekend immersion
7 days A classical week-long retreat
14+ days Teacher trainings and deep residencies

Pricing is shared on enquiry and depends on group size, dates, and inclusions. We hold dates by deposit; the rest is due closer to arrival.

A quick honesty

What we are not.

Mithila Yoga is a venue, not a school. We do not run retreats, sell teacher trainings, certify teachers, or recommend any particular lineage. Our work is to keep the place in good order — and to keep the heritage of this region honoured — so that teachers from elsewhere can do their own work here without distraction.

If you are looking for a retreat to attend as a student, please look at the calendars of teachers who have run programmes here. We're happy to point you to a recent one on request.
