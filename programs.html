Programmes & Residencies

Conversations we hope to host.

As the institution grows, Mithila Yoga will host scholar-residencies, public lectures, festivals tied to the Maithil calendar, and conversations between contemplative traditions. Below is the direction we are working towards — specific programmes will be announced as they take shape.

01

Scholar Residencies

Short-term residencies for researchers working on Maithil philosophy, tantra, ritual, or yoga. Two to eight weeks. Accommodation, archive access, and quiet for the work.

02

Public Lectures

Open lectures by scholars, teachers, and elders — in English, Hindi, and Maithili. Free to attend; recorded and made available afterwards.

03

Maithil Calendar

Programmes timed to Vivāha Pañcamī, Chhath, Sītā Navamī, and other moments of the region's living year — for visitors who want to be here when something is happening.

04

Conversations

Dialogues between Mithila's tradition and other contemplative streams — Buddhist, Sufi, Vaiṣṇava, modern secular thought. Small, careful, on the record.

Upcoming

The next programmes.

We don't publish a programme calendar until each programme is fully scoped. To be notified when programmes open, please write to us — we keep a simple mailing list and don't share it.

If you are a scholar, teacher, or institution that would like to propose a programme in collaboration with Mithila Yoga, we welcome conversations.
