While you're here

Madhubani's art villages, Janakpur (across the Nepal border, traditional birthplace of Sītā), Sitamarhi, and the temples of Darbhanga are all within easy reach.

When to come

The Maithil year.

The region has its own calendar of festivals — and many of these are intrinsically connected to Mithila Yoga's themes. Choosing your visit date around them, when possible, deepens the experience.

  • Vivāha Pañcamī (Mārgaśīrṣa Śukla 5, Nov–Dec) — annual commemoration of the marriage of Sītā and Rāma.
  • Chhath (Kārtika Śukla 6, Oct–Nov) — the four-day sun-meditation, observed on the banks of every river in the region.
  • Sītā Navamī (Vaiśākha Śukla 9, Apr–May) — the day of Sītā's birth, observed across Mithila.
  • Kojāgarī Pūrṇimā (Āśvina full moon, Oct) — full-moon vigil with devotional song.

Weather note: Mithila is hot in May–June and cool in December–January. October to March is the most comfortable season for visitors from outside the tropics.

Where we are

A village in the heart of Mithila.

Bishanpur is a small village in Madhubani district. The land around is part orchard, part field, part pond — the kind of countryside that has been farmed in roughly the same way for a thousand years. There is no airport, no shopping mall, no traffic. There are mosquitos in the evenings and the occasional power cut. Most of our visitors find this a feature, not a bug.

A simple map and detailed directions will be sent on confirmation of your visit. WhatsApp is the most reliable way to reach us on the day of arrival.
