## Re-sizing

Original files were downloaded full-resolution from Wikimedia.
Resized to max 1200px longest side at JPEG quality 80 using macOS
`sips` to reduce page weight. Originals can be re-downloaded from the
Wikimedia URLs above if higher resolution is needed.
