Get in touch

Write to us.

Whether you are a teacher planning a retreat, a scholar requesting archive access, a journalist, or simply curious — we'd be glad to hear from you. We answer all messages personally, usually within a few days.

Reach us at

The simplest ways.

  • Email · hello@mithilayoga.org
  • Phone / WhatsApp · [+91 number]
  • Address · Mithila Yoga, Bishanpur, Madhubani, Bihar [PIN]

For retreat enquiries, please mention your expected dates, group size, and the kind of programme you wish to lead.

What to expect

We are a small team and we answer enquiries ourselves. Most messages get a reply within two or three days. If you have not heard back after a week, please write again — sometimes a message lands in spam.

For complex enquiries — research access, group bookings, programme proposals — a phone or WhatsApp call after the initial email often helps. Mention your time zone and we'll propose a window.

Before you write

You may already find your answer.

If your question is about our spaces, please first see the retreat page. If it is about reaching us, see the visit page. If it is about what we hold in the archive, see the archives page. If it is a general question about Mithila's yoga tradition, the tradition page may help.

For media enquiries, please write to the email above with "Press" in the subject line. We will reply promptly.
